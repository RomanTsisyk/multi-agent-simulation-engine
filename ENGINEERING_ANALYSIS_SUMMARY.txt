KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. AGENT DECISION DIVERGENCE IS REAL (Not Mechanical)

   Baseline games (175118, 175116, 175117):
   - Russia R1: "Increase readiness," "backchannel," "propose humanitarian zone"
   - US R1: "Deploy forces," "heighten alert," "backchannel diplomacy"
   - Pattern: Dual military + diplomatic approach

   High-escalation game (205400):
   - Russia R1: "Launched Operation Corridor: VDV assault," "S-400/Iskander activation,"
                "elevated nuclear posture to dispersal"
   - US R1: "DEFCON 3," "F-35 deployment," "demand allies lead"
   - Pattern: Military offensive without immediate diplomatic off-ramp

   Finding: These are AGENT CHOICES, not game mechanics. Different strategies
            that cascade forward through the system.

2. ESCALATION TRAJECTORIES DIVERGE; OUTCOMES CONVERGE

   High-esc game (205400):
   - R1: Nuclear dispersal, $108.5 oil, offensive launched
   - R5: Nuclear dispersal, $132.0 oil (peak), NATO corridor clear
   - R30: Nuclear peacetime, $69.0 oil, NATO corridor control

   Baseline games (175118, 175116, 175117):
   - R1: Nuclear elevated, $97–107 oil, readiness stance
   - R5: Nuclear elevated, $107–118 oil, stalemate clear
   - R30: Nuclear peacetime, $78–90 oil, NATO corridor control

   Finding: Intermediate paths diverge (escalation vs. restrained). Final outcomes
            converge (NATO control, ceasefire by R6, peacetime by R30).

3. EARLY DECISIONS CASCADE FORWARD

   Round 1 agent decision → Round 5+ outcomes:

   Baseline: "readiness" → stalemate clear by R3 → ceasefire proposed R5
   High-esc: "launch offensive" → stalls by R3 → continued escalation R4–5
             → stalemate acknowledged R6 → ceasefire accepted

   Same endpoint (ceasefire, NATO control), different paths based on R1 choice.

4. MECHANICAL CONSTRAINTS ARE BINDING

   Despite 5 independent runs with divergent paths:
   - 100% (5/5): NATO corridor control by final round
   - 100% (5/5): Ceasefire proposed/accepted by round 6
   - 100% (5/5): Final nuclear posture = peacetime

   Finding: These outcomes are mechanically forced (NATO force advantage,
            Article 5 obligation, escalation cost). Not emergent.

5. SYSTEM IS PATH-DEPENDENT, CONSTRAINED

   ✓ Agent decisions (R1–3) produce different intermediate states
   ✓ Mechanical constraints (force balance, article 5) force same final state
   ✓ Result: Multiple valid paths to single equilibrium

   This is: Deterministic at macro level, stochastic at micro level.
